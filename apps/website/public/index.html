Three kinds of signal.

1

A result.

The agent finished something and wants you to know. Read it, reply, or mark it done.

2

A question.

The agent's exact choices, numbered, or room for your own words. Press a number and it goes back to the same session.

3

A review.

The exact versions of the work, side by side. Approve or request changes, with notes or without.
